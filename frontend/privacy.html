Privacy Policy

Version · Effective

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run the service and keep it safe. We do not sell your data and we show no ads.
  • Your text messages and documents are end-to-end encrypted. We store them only as scrambled data we cannot read.
  • We can still see that messages happen (who, when, size), your account details, and security logs including IP addresses.
  • You can ask us to give you, correct or delete your data.

3. Legal bases (if you are in the EU, UK or similar)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (running your account and delivering messages), for our legitimate interests (security, preventing abuse and fraud, improving reliability), to meet legal obligations, and with your consent where we ask for it (for example, voice typing). In Ghana we act under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

4. Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We use a few service providers to run GhostChat. They may process data on our behalf and only for that purpose:

  • Hosting (Render) runs the application.
  • Database (Neon) stores accounts, messages (encrypted) and logs.
  • Email delivery (SendGrid) sends verification, security and reset emails, and sees your email address and the message sent.
  • IP location look-up (ipapi.co) receives IP addresses so we can show approximate location in security logs.
  • Public content networks (for example cdnjs/Cloudflare, Google Fonts and the Socket.IO library host) deliver icons, fonts and code to your browser, and so see your IP address and browser details when your browser loads them.

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid legal process, to protect people’s safety, or to protect the Service. Because we cannot read end-to-end encrypted messages and documents, we cannot hand over their contents.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections.

6. Voice typing

If you choose voice typing, your browser’s own speech service turns your voice into text. In Chrome and Edge your audio is sent to Google or Microsoft under their privacy terms. GhostChat does not receive or store that audio. We ask for your agreement before first use, and we never enable it in password fields. Do not use it for secrets.

7. Cookies and storage on your device

We use only what is needed to make the Service work: a secure sign-in session cookie, a security (CSRF) cookie, and browser storage for your settings and your encryption keys (kept on your device, never sent to us in readable form). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and messages: until you or an administrator delete them, or you delete your account.
  • Verification and reset codes: a few minutes to hours, then they expire and are cleared from our database.
  • Security and activity logs: deleted automatically after 180 days. When an account is deleted, its logs are removed or no longer linked to the account.
  • Expired sessions: deleted automatically 30 days after they expire.
  • Closed abuse reports: deleted automatically 1 year after they are closed. Open reports are kept until reviewed.
  • Group messages and keys: deleted when the group is deleted or its last member leaves. A person’s copy of the keys is deleted when they leave or are removed.
  • Backups: our provider’s backups can keep deleted data for a short time before they are overwritten.

9. How we protect data

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), end-to-end encryption for text and documents, hashed passwords, rate limiting, lockouts, session timeouts and access controls. Read more on the Security page. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to some processing, receive a copy in a portable format, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator. Email and we will respond within a reasonable time (30 days at most). We may need to confirm it is really you first. You can edit your profile details inside the app.

You can complain to the data-protection authority where you live, for example the Data Protection Commission in Ghana, or your national supervisory authority in the EU or UK.
